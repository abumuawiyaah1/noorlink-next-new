# Makkah

## 1. al-Masjid al-Ḥarām
The heart of the journey. Ṭawāf, prayer, Zamzam, and Sa‘ī between Ṣafā and Marwah.  
**Etiquette:** Soft voice, no pushing, give way to the elderly and families, keep the pathways clear.

## 2. Ṣafā and Marwah
Part of the Umrah / Hajj rites for most pilgrims.  
**Etiquette:** Keep moving when the corridor is busy; do not sit in the walking lanes.

## 3. Jabal al-Nūr (Cave of Ḥirāʾ) — optional
A mountain climb some visitors make. It is **physically hard**, especially in heat.  
**Etiquette:** Only if you are fit; take water; do not leave rubbish; this is not a required rite of Umrah.

## 4. Mina, ʿArafāt, Muzdalifah (mainly Hajj)
Central to Hajj days. Your agency will schedule these.  
**Etiquette:** Follow your group strictly; heat and crowds need patience and hydration.

*(Umrah-only travelers usually do not need these stops.)*

# Madinah

## 1. al-Masjid an-Nabawī
Pray as much as you can with presence. Send ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ.  
**Etiquette:** No raising voices, no arguing for space, respect marked sections and prayer times.

## 2. The Rawḍah
A beloved, often crowded area.  
**Etiquette:** If you cannot enter without harming others, pray nearby with a good heart. Acceptance is with Allah — not with a photo.

## 3. Qubāʾ Mosque
Among the first mosques in Islam; many visit to pray two rakʿahs.  
**Etiquette:** Short visit; keep the mosque calm; return with your group if you came together.

## 4. Mount Uḥud
A place of history and reflection for the battle of Uḥud and the martyrs.  
**Etiquette:** Go with a clear plan; dress modestly; do not climb unsafely or litter.

## 5. Masjid al-Qiblatayn
Remembered for the change of the Qiblah. Often a short stop.  
**Etiquette:** Brief visit; do not block doors for pictures.

# Etiquette that protects the journey

## With Allah
- Keep your intention clean.  
- Prefer quiet dhikr over loud arguing.  
- If you make a mistake in a rite, ask a knowledgeable person calmly — do not panic.

## With people
- **Do not push** in ṭawāf, Sa‘ī, or the Rawḍah.  
- Help those who need space (elderly, wheelchair, parents with children).  
- Keep your group meeting point clear; do not block hotel lobbies.

## With the Kingdom’s rules
- Carry ID as required.  
- Follow Nusuk / Tawakkalna / official instructions when they apply to you.  
- Respect photography restrictions where posted.  
- Unlicensed guides who pressure you for money: walk away.

## With yourself
- Rest and hydrate — worship needs a clear mind.  
- Charge your phone and power bank before long days at the Haram.  
- Install your eSIM on Wi‑Fi **before** you fly so connection is one less stress on arrival.
