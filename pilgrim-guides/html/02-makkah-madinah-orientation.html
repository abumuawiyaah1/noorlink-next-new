Makkah — al-Masjid al-Ḥarām

Aim for the Kaʿbah at the centre. Ṭawāf moves around it. Stay gentle in the crowd.

Kaʿbah & ṭawāf

Follow floor markings and staff. Do not push toward the Black Stone.

Zamzam

Available in marked areas. Drink with a good intention.

Ṣafā & Marwah

Sa‘ī corridors are signed in Arabic and English. Keep walking lanes clear.

If you feel lost

Pause at a quieter edge, open your hotel name, ask mosque staff or your group.

al-Masjid an-Nabawī — soft voices, especially near the Rawḍah.

Madinah — al-Masjid an-Nabawī

The Prophet’s Mosque

Large courtyards and halls. Follow marked men’s and women’s sections.

The Rawḍah

Often timed and crowded. If you cannot enter calmly, pray nearby with a good heart.

Nearby visits

Qubāʾ, Uḥud, and Qiblatayn are common short stops — go with a clear plan.

Hotels at night

Streets can look similar. Note your hotel tower colour or name before sunset.
