## Du‘a for travel

**Arabic**  
سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ

**Transliteration**  
Subḥāna-lladhī sakhkhara lanā hādhā wa mā kunnā lahu muqrinīn, wa innā ilā rabbinā la-munqalibūn.

**Meaning**  
Glory be to the One who has subjected this to us, and we could not have done it by ourselves — and to our Lord we will surely return.  
*(Qur’an 43:13–14 — often recited when setting out.)*

## Entering the sacred state / Talbiyah (Umrah & Hajj)

**Arabic**  
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

**Transliteration**  
Labbayka Allāhumma labbayk. Labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk. Inna al-ḥamda wa-n-ni‘mata laka wa-l-mulk. Lā sharīka lak.

**Meaning**  
Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am — You have no partner — here I am. Truly all praise, favour, and sovereignty are Yours. You have no partner.

## At the Haram — starting Ṭawāf

Many begin with the Name of Allah and takbīr when approaching the Black Stone area (as taught by their school / guide). Keep your voice calm; do not push.

**Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone (widely reported)**  

**Arabic**  
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

**Transliteration**  
Rabbanā ātinā fī d-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fī l-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ‘adhāba n-nār.

**Meaning**  
Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.  
*(Qur’an 2:201)*

---

## After Ṭawāf / at Zamzam

Drink Zamzam with a good intention. You may make any sincere du‘a — for yourself, your family, the Ummah, and an accepted pilgrimage.

---

## During Sa‘ī (Ṣafā and Marwah)

Remember that Sa‘ī is part of the rites. Many recite general dhikr and Qur’an. At Ṣafā, the Prophet ﷺ faced the Qiblah and made du‘a (follow what your teacher taught). Keep moving with gentleness in the crowd.

---

## In al-Masjid an-Nabawī (Madinah)

Send ṣalawāt upon the Prophet ﷺ. In the Rawḍah, if you are able to reach it without harming others, make quiet du‘a. If the area is crowded, your prayer anywhere in the mosque is still a great blessing — do not rush or push.

**Sending peace upon the Prophet ﷺ**  
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ…

*(Use the full form you know from prayer.)*

## A short general du‘a (anytime)

**Arabic**  
رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي

**Transliteration**  
Rabbi shraḥ lī ṣadrī wa yassir lī amrī.

**Meaning**  
My Lord, expand my chest for me and make my affair easy for me.  
*(Qur’an 20:25–26)*

---

## When you return home

Thank Allah for safety. Ask that your pilgrimage be accepted, and that you keep the good habits you started.
