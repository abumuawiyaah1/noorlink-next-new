# Makkah — al-Masjid al-Ḥarām

## What you are aiming for
- **The Kaʿbah** — centre of the Haram; ṭawāf is around it.  
- **The Black Stone (al-Ḥajar al-Aswad)** — marks a starting point for many for ṭawāf; do not push to touch it.  
- **Maqām Ibrāhīm** — near the Kaʿbah; prayer area often busy.  
- **Zamzam** — available in marked areas; drink with a good intention.  
- **Ṣafā and Marwah** — the two hills for Sa‘ī (connected corridors / floors inside the mosque complex).

## Orientation in plain words
Think of the Haram as a large circle of worship around the Kaʿbah:

```
                    [ upper floors / more space ]
                              |
        [ Marwah side ] --- KAʿBAH --- [ Ṣafā side ]
                              |
                    [ prayer halls & exits ]
```

- **Inside:** follow floor markings and staff for ṭawāf direction (counter-clockwise around the Kaʿbah).  
- **Ṣafā → Marwah:** Sa‘ī has a clear start/end; signs are usually in Arabic and English.  
- **Exits:** hotel areas sit in different directions (Ajyad, Ibrahim Al Khalil, etc.). Ask your hotel name at the info desk if you are lost — staff are used to this.

## Calm tips near the Haram
- Peak times = slower movement. Leave early for prayer.  
- Keep your group WhatsApp and hotel name saved on your phone.  
- Wheelchairs and family groups need extra space — make room.  
- Photography rules and restricted zones: follow posted signs.

## If you feel lost
1. Stop in a quieter edge of a prayer hall.  
2. Open your hotel name in Contacts / Notes.  
3. Ask mosque staff or your group leader — do not follow strangers into cars.

# Madinah — al-Masjid an-Nabawī

## What you are aiming for
- **The Prophet’s Mosque** — vast courtyards and indoor halls.  
- **The Green Dome** — landmark above the Prophet’s ﷺ resting place (view with respect; follow access rules).  
- **The Rawḍah** — a blessed area between the pulpit and the chamber; often timed entry / crowding.  
- **Women’s and men’s sections** — follow marked doors and times.

## Orientation in plain words

```
        [ Courtyards / shade umbrellas ]
                    |
     [ Prayer halls ] -- [ Rawḍah / front areas ]
                    |
        [ Outer streets · hotels · markets ]
```

- Hotels are often a short walk around the mosque — streets can look similar at night; note your hotel tower colour / name.  
- For **Rawḍah**: follow official timed entry if required; pushing is not worth the harm. Prayer elsewhere in the mosque remains a great gift.

## Nearby places (short visits — see also Places PDF)
- **Qubāʾ Mosque** — often reached by short ride / group trip.  
- **Uhud** — outside the centre; go with a plan, not alone at odd hours if you are unfamiliar.  
- **Qiblatayn** — short stop for many groups.

## Calm tips in Madinah
- The city is generally quieter than Makkah’s Haram rush — still protect your belongings.  
- Friday and peak seasons fill the mosque early.  
- Dress and behaviour: modest, soft voices, especially near the Rawḍah.
