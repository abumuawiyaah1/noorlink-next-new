# NoorLink Insider — Editorial Brief

## Promise
Useful travel connectivity for practical travelers: destination guides, real tips, Hajj & Umrah timing, and occasional deal alerts — without inbox noise.

## Cadence
**One email per month** (same week each month, e.g. first Tuesday).

## Audience
**One list for everyone.** Every issue should feel relevant to leisure travelers *and* pilgrimage travelers. Mix sections; never force a reader into a segment.

## Mix per issue (default)
1. Short opener (why this month matters)
2. Destination / places guide (1 focus)
3. Connectivity tip (install, hotspot, data habits)
4. Seasonal Hajj & Umrah note (short when not peak; deeper near seasons)
5. Deal / new-route alert (honest, optional — skip if nothing real)
6. One clear CTA (destination, Hajj page, or device check)

## Tone
Practical, calm, trustworthy. Travel advisor energy — not hype. Prefer “install before you fly” over “limited time!!!”

## What we do not do
- Weekly promo blasts
- Fake urgency
- Overpromising coverage or speeds
- Dense walls of text

## File map
- `YEAR-CALENDAR.md` — 12-month plan
- `issues/` — full ready-to-send copy for each month
