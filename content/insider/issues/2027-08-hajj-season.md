## Connectivity tip — The “day before departure” ritual
Night before you fly:
1. Confirm eSIM shows as installed  
2. Toggle it off until landing (optional, depending on your setup)  
3. Screenshot plan details / QR email  
4. Charge power bank  
5. Download offline maps for day-one areas
