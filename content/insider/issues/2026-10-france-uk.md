## Opener
October is made for long weekends. This issue focuses on France and the UK — plus a reminder that short trips still deserve a proper data plan.

## Destination guide — France & United Kingdom
**France**  
Paris works best when maps and transit apps stay online. Museums, cafés, and day trips (Versailles, day trains) are smoother when you’re not hunting café Wi‑Fi.

**United Kingdom**  
London’s Tube + walking days burn battery and data. Keep hotspot for a companion phone or laptop on layover days.

**Practical pairing**  
Flying open-jaw (e.g. London in, Paris out) is common. Check whether single-country plans or a Europe regional plan fits your route better — borders shouldn’t mean a new SIM.

## Connectivity tip — Hotspot is not “extra”
Most NoorLink plans include hotspot. Use it for:
- A travel partner’s phone in a pinch  
- Laptop boarding passes / hotel check-in  
- Sharing maps in a car or train  

Don’t assume hotel Wi‑Fi will be fast, safe, or available when you need it.

## Hajj & Umrah note — Pack light digitally
Even on a short Umrah, you’ll want:
- Maps for Makkah / Madinah areas you care about  
- Group chat that works on arrival  
- A plan installed before you leave home  

Airport kiosks add stress. Install early.
