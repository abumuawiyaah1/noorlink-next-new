## Destination guide — Saudi Arabia (Umrah focus)
You’re not there to hunt SIM shops. You’re there for worship, family, and logistics.

**Connectivity that helps**
- Group coordination on WhatsApp  
- Maps between hotel, Haram areas, and transport  
- Hotspot for a companion device  

**Install before you fly**  
Do it at home on stable Wi‑Fi. On arrival, enable the eSIM data line and keep moving.

## Connectivity tip — Pilgrimage settings checklist
1. Confirm eSIM compatibility  
2. Install NoorLink plan before departure  
3. Name the line clearly in Settings  
4. Enable data roaming for the eSIM only when you land  
5. Keep hotspot ready for family sharing
