## Connectivity tip — Holiday travel habits
Airports are crowded in December. Reduce friction:
1. Install eSIM at home  
2. Label the line “NoorLink Travel” in settings  
3. On landing, enable data roaming for that line only  
4. Keep hotspot off until you need it (battery)
